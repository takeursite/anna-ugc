Frequently Asked Questions

Where are you based?

I’m based in Armenia
and available for remote
content projects with
brands in Europe and the US.

How does product
delivery work?

We confirm shipping options
and costs before booking.
If your product is available
in Armenia, a pre-approved local
purchase may also be possible.

What does a quote
include?

Each quote specifies the number
of assets, revisions, timelines and
usage rights. Paid advertising,
raw footage and additional
versions are agreed separately
where applicable.

Do you create content
in English?

I create visual content with
English on-screen text, without
speaking to camera. Voice-over
options, including AI-assisted
voice-over, can be agreed in
advance. Project communication
is handled in writing.

Do you post the
content on your own
social media?

My core service is content
creation for your brand’s
channels. Posting on my own
accounts is not included
unless separately agreed.
